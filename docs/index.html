01 / PROFILE

Physics across scales, connecting theory and computation.

My work sits where cosmology, high-energy astrophysics, and scientific computing meet.

I use hydrodynamic and N-body simulations, Monte Carlo transport, and statistical analysis to connect small-scale physical processes with the formation of stars, haloes, and black holes.

I am especially interested in questions that demand both physical interpretation and careful numerical validation: how primordial gas collapses, how dark matter responds, how photons gain energy in hot plasmas, and how resolution affects the conclusions we draw.

02 / SELECTED WORK

Research driven by simulations, not shortcuts.

Current directions and research code, organized by the physics questions they address.

POPULATION III.1 · DARK MATTER · ONGOING01

Dark matter around the first protostars

Jonathan C. Tan · Naoki Yoshida · Shingo Hirano

High-resolution cosmological zoom-in simulations of primordial minihaloes, following coupled gas and dark-matter evolution into runaway collapse. I investigate inner density slopes, adiabatic contraction and possible implications for massive black-hole seeds.

GADGET-3SPHN-bodyPrimordial chemistryHPC
COMPTONIZATION · POLARIZATION02

Monte Carlo radiative transfer in structured plasmas

Alexandra Veledina

Monte Carlo modelling of relativistic Compton scattering in accreting plasmas, comparing homogeneous electron populations with structured plasmoid and X-point configurations. Diagnostics include spectra, angular distributions and polarization.

Monte CarloMPIKlein–NishinaPolarization
HALO ASSEMBLY · MERGER TREES03

Merger rates from Phi-4096 and Shin-Uchuu

Tomoaki Ishiyama

Measurements of halo merger rates across complementary simulation volumes and mass ranges, using counting, completeness and overlap checks to separate astrophysical trends from resolution-dependent systematics.

PythonHDF5Merger treesStatistics
SIMULATION INFRASTRUCTURE04

Modernizing GADGET snapshot I/O

Research collaboration · Shingo Hirano

Reactivation of HDF5 initial-condition, snapshot and restart support in GADGET3-DAWN while retaining compatibility with older binary formats, with validation of MPI and restart edge cases.

CMPIHDF5Reproducibility
ACCRETION PHYSICS05

Relativistic flows and black-hole growth

Indranil Chattopadhyay · Sandeep Kumar Kataria
