01 / PROFILE

Physics across scales, connecting theory and computation.

My work sits where cosmology, high-energy astrophysics, and scientific computing meet.

I use hydrodynamic and N-body simulations, Monte Carlo transport, and statistical analysis to connect small-scale physical processes with the formation of stars, haloes, and black holes.

I am especially interested in questions that demand both physical interpretation and careful numerical validation: how primordial gas collapses, how dark matter responds, how photons gain energy in hot plasmas, and how resolution affects the conclusions we draw.

CHALMERS · SUMMER 2026 · POPULATION III.101

Primordial minihalo collapse & dark-matter cusp evolution

WITH Jonathan C. Tan · Shingo Hirano · Naoki Yoshida

MY CONTRIBUTIONS
  • Ran high-resolution cosmological zoom-in calculations of metal-free minihalo collapse using modified GADGET with primordial chemistry and radiative cooling.
  • Measured resolved inner dark-matter density profiles across multiple haloes and quantified cusp steepening and halo-to-halo variation.
  • Compared simulation trends with adiabatic-contraction predictions; investigated the roles of gas thermal history, rotation and collapse dynamics.

GADGET-4 primordial chemistry & adaptive refinement

WITH Jonathan C. Tan · Shingo Hirano · Naoki Yoshida

MY CONTRIBUTIONS
  • Developed and tested a GADGET-4 implementation of non-equilibrium primordial chemistry and cooling routines.
  • Implemented and examined on-the-fly particle splitting to extend dynamic mass resolution.
  • Investigated numerical treatments to avoid artificial density discontinuities at interfaces between particles of unequal mass.

NORDITA · SUMMER 2026 · RADIATION TRANSPORT03

Monte Carlo Comptonization & polarized radiative transfer

WITH Alexandra Veledina

MY CONTRIBUTIONS
  • Built a modular Monte Carlo Comptonization framework for hot plasmas with spatially varying electron populations.
  • Extended photon propagation to polarized scattering, angular binning and Stokes-parameter diagnostics in anisotropic geometries.
